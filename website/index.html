Planned features

Voice-first

Push-to-talk with a male voice, using cloud speech where a free provider supports it.

Cloud intelligence

Groq, Gemini and OpenRouter through your own free-tier API keys. No AI models are downloaded to your phone.

Memory & skills

Opt-in local memory and custom skills you can inspect, edit and delete.

Documents & vision

Only files and images you select are sent, after you confirm.

Actions

Allow-listed actions such as timers and drafts, always shown for confirmation first.

Privacy controls

No tracking SDKs. Microphone, camera and screen access only in sessions you start.

Cloud data handling

ZAEEM AI processes content in the cloud. Whatever you send goes to the provider you choose, over TLS, and that provider can read it. Free tiers may retain or use inputs under their own terms (Google, for example, states free-tier content may be used to improve its products). Do not send confidential sources, patient data or classified material. Deleting data in the app does not guarantee deletion at the provider.

Free-tier limits

Free allowances can change, be throttled or disappear. Nothing here promises unlimited use, uptime or zero cost. Each integration is enabled only after its free eligibility is verified.

Requirements

Android (arm64) and an internet connection for AI features. A provider API key you create yourself.
